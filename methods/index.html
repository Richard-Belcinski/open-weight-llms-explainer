Notable Gap: No Precision/Recall/F1

Balanced accuracy hides the tradeoff between missed findings and hallucinated values. For clinical deployment, missing a tumour finding (recall) is far worse than a false positive (precision). A model scoring 0.89 could be missing 5% of findings or hallucinating 5% — very different failure modes.

Aggregation

All metrics aggregated via macro-average — equal weight per variable, regardless of how many reports contain it. This prevents high-frequency variables from dominating results.
