Key Finding

LLMs Exceed Human Agreement in 3 of 5 Cases

Best LLM scores surpass human inter-rater agreement for melanomas, soft-tissue EN, and soft-tissue NL — but what does that actually mean?

Annotation Gap

Two Use Cases Have No IRA Baseline

Colorectal liver metastases and liver tumours had single annotators — ceiling performance is unknowable.

Statistical Caveat

CIs Overlap in "Exceeds" Cases

Two of the three "LLM exceeds" findings have substantially overlapping confidence intervals — the differences may not be statistically significant.

Interpretation

IRA as a Ceiling vs Floor

If human raters disagree on 24% of melanoma annotations, is 0.76 IRA the ceiling models should aim for — or a floor they should beat?

Data Quality

Uneven Rater Counts Across Use Cases

Sarcomas had 9 raters, melanomas had 2, and two use cases had just 1 — the IRA estimates vary widely in reliability.
