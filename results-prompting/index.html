Recommendation

Few-Shot: The Practical Sweet Spot

+5.1% improvement at 4.5s/report — roughly 40% of prompt graph's gain at 12% of the time cost.

Cost Concern

Prompt Graph: Diminishing Returns?

+12.7% is the highest gain, but at 37.6s/report it requires manual graph definition per use case and isn't automatable.

Uncertainty

Chain-of-Thought: High Variance

+8.2% average improvement, but the CI [2.2–14.2%] is very wide — the effect may be negligible for some use cases.

Baseline

Zero-Shot Is Surprisingly Decent

The baseline performance without any examples is already clinically useful for some use cases — adding complexity isn't always necessary.

Trade-off

Self-Consistency: Modest Gains, Real Cost

+6.1% at 15.9s/report — requires multiple inference passes for marginal improvement over few-shot.

Critique

Strategy × Model Interactions Not Tested

The LME model assumes strategy effects are uniform across models — but some models may respond very differently to specific strategies.
