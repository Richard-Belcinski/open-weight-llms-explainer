Open-Weight LLMs for Medical Report Extraction

Spaanderman et al. | arXiv 2511.10658 | Interactive Explainer

Key Findings

Five takeaways from 15 models, 6 strategies, and 4,060 clinical reports

1

Clinical-Grade Extraction

Open-weight LLMs achieve clinical-grade extraction across diseases, languages, and institutions

2

24B Matches 685B

General-purpose 24B models match 685B models with optimized prompting — size doesn't matter above a threshold

3

Medical Models Are Counterproductive

Medical-specialized models (Med42, OpenBioLLM, MedGemma) underperform general-purpose models for extraction tasks

4

Both Model and Prompting Matter — Modestly

LLM choice explains 18-37% and prompting 0.2-35% of variance — overlapping ranges, with neither clearly dominant over the other

5

Task-Specific Factors Dominate

45-80% of variance is unexplained by model or strategy — task-specific characteristics drive performance
