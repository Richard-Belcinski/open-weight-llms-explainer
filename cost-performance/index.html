Key Insights

Pareto Champion

Mistral Small 3.1 (24B) sits on the Pareto frontier with the best score-to-size ratio. It matches or beats models 10-28x its size.

Diminishing Returns

Above ~24B parameters, adding more capacity yields marginal gains. The large (>150B) category averages 0.78 — barely above small models at 0.77.

The 4B Cliff

Below 4B parameters, performance drops sharply. Tiny models average 0.57 — a 20-point gap from their larger peers, suggesting a minimum capacity threshold.
